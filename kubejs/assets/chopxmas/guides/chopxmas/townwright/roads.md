# Roads, Bridges and the Plaza

## Roads

Every building gets a road to the rest of town. As the town fills in, main roads run out from the Town Hall and side streets reach the huts further away.

* **Better roads over time:** road near a levelled-up building takes a finer surface. Roads only ever improve.
* **Steps:** where a road climbs a block, the edge becomes a step, so nobody has to jump.
* **The road crew:** one or two workers push a wheelbarrow out from the Town Hall and lay main roads, plaza streets and upgrades block by block, then walk home. A hut's own door road is laid at once. You can turn this off on the Town settings tab, and roads then appear all at once.
* Town roads are kept clear of snow.
* Townwright only touches roads it laid itself. Your own paths are left alone.

## Road Stakes

Want a road somewhere yourself? Craft **Road Stakes** (orange dye over 3 sticks over paper). The town owner and officers can use them:

* Right-click two spots and the town paves a road between them.
* Crouch and right-click to pave a road from that spot to the town.
* Right-click inside a claimed plot or a player house: it warns first. Right-click again within 15 seconds to pave a road from it to the town.

The road crew lays these like any other town road.

## Bridges

Roads over water or gaps become bridges with railings, and longer ones get arches and piers underneath. Their look follows your town's material set and gets finer as the buildings nearby level up.

## The Plaza

In a Plaza town the plaza is the heart of town, with the Town Hall at one end. On the Plans tab, press **Build it** on the **Town plaza** note and the town lays it out: paved paths, a Christmas tree square, market stalls on one side, a park with benches on the other, and lanterns. It grows with your Town Hall. The plaza, the walk to the Town Hall and the university plot are levelled to the hall's ground.

## Lights

Until your first guard tower or barracks stands, lanterns go on dark ground around the hall and the build sites so monsters can't spawn among your citizens. They stay afterwards as street lights.
