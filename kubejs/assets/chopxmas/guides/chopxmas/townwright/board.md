# The Planning Board

Right-click your **Town Planner** to open the Planning Board, a cork board with three tabs: **Town settings**, **Plans** and **Plot map**.

## Plans

* **Would like to build:** what the town wants to build next. Press **Yes** to build it, or **No** to leave it out for now. After Yes it reads **Building next**.
* **Or the town could use:** up to 3 other buildings the town needs right now. Hover one to see why ("Build this instead: ..."). Click it to build that **instead**. The plan's own pick comes back afterwards.
* **Skipped:** click a skipped building to let the town propose it again. Skipping a cook, farmer, fisher, warehouse, courier or homes shows a warning first.
* **Coming up:** the next steps of the plan. Drag one onto another to swap their order; **Plan's own order** undoes it.
* **Notes:** **Town plan** (your layout, sealed once 5 buildings stand), **Town plaza** and **Decorations** (Build it or Another; auto, pick or off).
* The status slip shows the treasury, for example "Treasury: 42 emeralds left".
* If a build is stuck, a line explains why.

A building you said Yes to is built before anything new is proposed. A Builder's Hut can't be skipped or swapped, because everything else needs builders.

## Town settings

A card for every choice: building looks, road surface, bridges, snow clearing at build sites, lanterns until the guards arrive, progress markers over building sites, foundation walls, the road crew and more. Click a chip to change it.

## Plot map

A paper map of your town: roads, the plaza, buildings, and a tag on every plot. See **Plots and Your Own Builds**.

## Who decides

Only the town's **owner and officers** can say Yes or No or change settings. Anyone can look. When a deciding player logs in, the board reminds them of any proposal that is waiting.

## What gets proposed

Early on you get builders and Town Hall upgrades, so your money goes where it matters. After that the town asks for what it needs: homes when every bed is taken, a hospital when citizens are sick, guards, a farmer, a restaurant, a warehouse and courier, a tavern when spirits are low, and a school. A thriving town (15 or more citizens, happiness 7 or more) is offered the rest of the building catalogue.

You can always place huts yourself. Townwright works around them.
