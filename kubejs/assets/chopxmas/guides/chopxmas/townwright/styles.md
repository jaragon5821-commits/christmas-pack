# Town Styles

Every town picks a **style**. It is the first question in Town setup (under **Town layout**), and you can change it later on the **Town settings** tab: the card is **Town style**. A change only affects new growth. What's already built stays as it is.

| Style | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | A dense town. Buildings sit close together, bumpy ground is levelled, and banks get walls. |
| **Balanced** | The middle way, and the default. This is how towns grew before styles existed. |
| **Nature** | A spread-out, rural town. It builds on gentle ground (hilltops too), moves as little earth as it can, and leaves slopes instead of walls. |

Anything you set by hand on the Town settings tab, such as spacing or banks, still wins over the style.

## Streets follow the style

New main and plaza streets are levelled before they are laid, so you don't get a road that bumps up and down every block:

* Each row across a street is level, and the street climbs or drops at most one block at a time.
* **Structure** cuts and fills the most to keep streets flat, with walls where it leaves a face. **Balanced** does a bit less, also with walls. **Nature** barely touches the ground and leaves gentle earth slopes.
* Where a hill is too steep for that, the road climbs as a straight flight of steps. If even that can't work, the street just follows the ground and the board says "too steep to grade".
* Banks at a bridge are cut into a ramp, so the bridge connects instead of stopping at a steep edge.
* The road crew does the digging and filling block by block, so you can watch it happen.
* Streets that are already laid keep their blocks.

## Facing the plaza

Buildings around the plaza always face it. If a plot near the plaza can't have its door on the plaza side, the town picks another plot instead of turning its back on the square.
