# Fires

## Nobody rebuilds a burning house

While a building is on fire, the town doesn't send builders in. Repairs, upgrades and new work for it wait, and any open order for it is pulled. The Plans tab shows **On fire: repairs wait**. Once the fire has been out for a full minute, the work is filed again by itself.

A fire burning on a fire source, like a hearth or netherrack, is decoration and doesn't count.

## The fire squad

A town with a **Barracks** (level 1 or more) has a fire squad. When a building really catches fire, the road crew drops its road work, grabs a water bucket and puts the fire out. Fires always come before roads.

* Each bucket puts out the fire around it, a couple of blocks each way.
* Only the fire goes. No building blocks are broken.
* Hearth fires are left alone.

No barracks yet? Then nobody fights the fire. Put it out yourself, quickly.
