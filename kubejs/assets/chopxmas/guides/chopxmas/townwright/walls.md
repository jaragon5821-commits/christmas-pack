# Walls

## The town wall

Once the town needs defending, the Plans tab shows "Would like to build: the town wall". Press **Yes** when you're ready, or let other buildings go first.

When the wall is up, the **Town wall** note shows how far along it is, with these chips:

* **Upgrade:** the builders go round again and raise every piece one level. The wall can't be a higher level than the Town Hall.
* **Retry N** (N = pieces left out): tries again any piece that couldn't be built (water, steep ground, something in the way).
* **Take down:** the builders remove it piece by piece.
* **Move out:** when buildings crowd the wall in a growing town, the builders raise the new stretch first and only then take the old one down, so the town is never left open. In a Plaza or Walled Ring town the first wall stays as the inner ring, and an **Outer wall** can be added later.

Farms, foresters and mines far from town stay outside the walls. A road is laid from each gate to the town's roads.

## Foundation walls

Where the ground drops away at the edge of a finished building, a wall goes up from solid ground to hold the earth in, flush with the floor. This keeps hillside towns tidy. You can turn it off on the Town settings tab.

The **banks** setting (slope, wall or off; slope by default) handles the ground behind a building: a bank rising more than 2 blocks is cut back to a slope you can walk up.
