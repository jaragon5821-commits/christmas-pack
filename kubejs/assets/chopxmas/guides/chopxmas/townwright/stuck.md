# When Something Seems Stuck

Type `/townwright status` while standing in your town. It says in plain words what your town is waiting for.

| The status says | What to do |
|---|---|
| waiting for materials ... Treasury: 0 emeralds | Pay emeralds in at the Town Hall, or bring the materials. |
| the builders' trickle buys materials a little at a time | Normal. Builders buy a little every minute. |
| its racks are full: moved ... to the town hall | Normal. Dug-up clutter goes to the Town Hall's racks. |
| its racks are full and nothing could be moved out | Empty the Builder's Hut or Town Hall racks. |
| It is raining or snowing, so citizens shelter | Wait. Workers stop in bad weather. |
| the plan board proposes X and waits | Say Yes, No or swap it on the Planning Board. |
| no buildable dry plot for it in reach yet | Clear some land, grow the town's claim, or place that hut yourself. |
| the plan waits on the hall | The Town Hall upgrade is due. It needs a built Builder's Hut and materials. |

**Stuck colonists:** a colonist that can't get any closer to where it's going for a while is moved there for you.

**Still stuck?** Place the hut yourself. Townwright always works around what you build.
