# Reading an Animal

## Nature

Every animal has its own nature, made of five sides: **Courage**, **Social**, **Temper**, **Energy** and **Curiosity**. Many also have a quirk or two, like Cuddly, Skittish, Glutton, Show-off or Night Owl. Two hares can be nothing alike.

Everything an animal does leans on its nature. A brave one walks right up to you; a shy one watches from far off. Nothing is impossible, only more or less likely. Natures drift slowly with how the animal is treated.

## Moods and faces

* **Mood bubbles:** a small bubble over its head shows a strong mood. A calm, content animal shows nothing, which is a good sign.
* **Faces:** the pack's own creatures show their mood on their face: happy, sad, sleepy, scared or cross.
* **Sleepy Zs:** a sleeping animal puffs a little Z now and then.

## The Temperament Spyglass

Craft one from a **spyglass** and a **Pet Treat**. Hold right-click and aim at an animal. Its thoughts drift up beside your crosshair, hinting at whether it is friendly, shy or one to keep an eye on. It works on wild animals and pets alike.
