# When an Animal Comes to You

Sometimes an animal reaches out to you. It might walk right up, or watch from a distance and creep closer. There are two ways it tells you what it has in mind.

## Picture bubbles

Simple needs show as a small picture bubble over the animal:

* **A red cross:** it's hurt and needs help.
* **Its food:** it's hungry. The bubble shows the actual food it eats.
* **A Pet Treat:** your pet is cross with you, and a treat would mend it.
* **A little roof:** it's cold and wet and wants shelter.
* **A hand with a heart:** it wants to be petted.
* **A ball:** it wants to play.

Do the thing it asks and the bubble pops into hearts.

## Your own thoughts

Everything else shows on **your** screen as your own thought, with the animal's portrait. For example: "Pip keeps bouncing around me. I think it wants to play!" There is nothing to press. You answer by what you do, and doing nothing is an answer too.

How long it waits depends on the want and the animal. A calm, sociable one gives you half a minute or more. One that wants a swim is off after a few seconds.

## What it might want, and what to do

* **Watching you from a distance:** stand still for a few seconds, or crouch, and it creeps closer. Run at it and a shy one bolts.
* **Curious, up close:** stand still, or crouch and pet it, and it sniffs your hand. Hit it and it slinks off.
* **Rest with you:** stay still close by for a few seconds, or crouch and pet it, and it curls up beside you. Walk off and it sighs and gets up.
* **Tired of following:** stop near it for a few seconds, or crouch and pet it, and it naps. Keep going and it trudges after you.
* **Fetch:** throw a toy, or drop (<KeyBind id="key.drop" />) a stick or a bone, and it brings it back to your feet.
* **Warmth:** lead it near a campfire or a hearth.
* **Comfort when scared:** crouch right next to it. Sprinting, fighting nearby or leaving keeps it shaking.
* **A play fight:** tap it with an **empty** hand. The tap does no damage. Walk well away and it's let down.
* **A challenge:** hold your ground facing it for a few seconds and it backs down. Back off and it struts, sure it won. Hit it and it gives a warning lunge, and remembers.
* **"Go away":** back off a good way (about 8 blocks) and it settles. Walk at it and it gives a warning lunge.
* **"Come and see":** it trots off right away. Follow it and it finds you something. Fall far behind and it gives up.
* **A gift:** it drops something small at your feet. Pick it up and it's happy. Leave it and it takes it back, a bit hurt.
* **A swim, a friend to visit, a patrol:** it soon goes off on its own. Crouch near it and it stays with you instead.
* **Sunbathing:** leave it to nap in the sun, or crouch and pet it for a belly rub.
* **Jealous:** crouch and pet it, and all is well. Pet your other pet instead and it stomps off.
* **A bite of your snack:** hand it some food, or drop a bite, and it gobbles it. Eat your own food in front of it and you get a dramatic sigh.
* **Hide and seek:** it runs off to hide. Find it and get right next to it for a "found you!" If you don't, it pops out after a minute.
* **Showing off:** it hops about. Watch it for a few seconds and it's proud. Look away and it deflates.

## Good to know

* Doing the right thing builds trust.
* Ignoring a want never makes a wild animal hold it against you.
* A challenge or a "go away" handled the wrong way gets a warning lunge that stops short. It never actually bites a player.
* Some animals are bold and some are shy. A shy one may need you to stand still, or to back away, before it dares come closer. If you sprint at it, it runs.
