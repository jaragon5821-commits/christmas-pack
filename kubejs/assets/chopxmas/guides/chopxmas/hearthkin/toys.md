# Toys and Music

## Toys

Throw a toy and animals may chase it, bat it about or carry it off. Punch a toy to kick it; sneak-punch or right-click it to pick it up. Pets you own may fetch a toy you throw or kick and drop it at your feet. Toys are never used up.

* **Yarn Ball**
* **Soft Ball**
* **Chew Bone**
* **Squeaky Gingerbread**
* **Toy Carrot:** a squeak toy.
* **Feather Wand:** wave it and watch them pounce.

Each toy is made for certain animals, who love it most. Any other animal still plays with it, just a little less. Tossing a toy near a shy animal is also a gentle way to make friends.

## Dancing

Play a record in a jukebox and tameable animals nearby may dance (about half of them; lively, silly ones more often) until the song ends. They also dance when a player nearby plays an instrument, and stop a few seconds after the music stops.
