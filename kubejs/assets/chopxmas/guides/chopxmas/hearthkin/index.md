# Hearthkin Guide

**Hearthkin** gives the pack's animals a life of their own. Every animal with a personality has a nature, moods that come and go, and a memory for how you treat it. Wild animals, farm animals and pets all count.

You will never see a number. You read an animal the way you would a real one: by how it acts, how it looks at you and what it asks for.

* **[Reading an Animal](reading.md):** nature, moods, faces and thought bubbles.
* **[When an Animal Comes to You](wants.md):** what its bubble is asking, and how to answer.
* **[Being Kind (and Not)](kindness.md):** petting, treats, grudges and soft spots.
* **[Taming](taming.md):** every animal can become a friend, one step at a time.
* **[Your Bond](bond.md):** how close you and your pet have grown.
* **[Toys and Music](toys.md):** playtime.
* **[The Field Guide](field_guide.md):** your pet journal.

Hearthkin never touches villagers, MCA people or colonists. It is only about animals.
