# The Field Guide

Craft the **Field Guide** from a **book** and a **Pet Treat** (new players start with one in their Bookbag). Right-click to open it.

## Your pets

Every pet you tame gets a page with **Today**, **Its nature**, **Memories** and **Our bond**, plus a **Rename** button. When you tame a pet, a card asks if you want to name it now or later.

## Taming notes

While you are taming an animal, each step you finish is written down, with the clue for the next one.

## Creatures

The first time you get a good look at one of the pack's rare creatures, it gets its own entry, and a line at the bottom of your screen tells you so.

**Rumours:** old notes and torn pages turn up in chests around the world. Find one and the creature it describes shows up in your Field Guide as **Rumoured**: a silhouette and what you've heard. The full entry opens when you see the creature for yourself.
