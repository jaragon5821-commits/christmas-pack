# Taming

Any animal with a personality can become a friend, but never by accident. Taming is a short chain of steps, and you always finish it yourself.

## How it starts

Give the animal a pat (hold Left Alt and right-click it), or do what your thought suggests when it comes to you. Then your own thoughts give you a clue about what to try next, using the animal's name. Each step you finish adds a note in your **Field Guide**, and the next clue appears.

Most animals go like this: a pat to let it get to know you, then food it likes (or a toy it likes) 2 times, a few seconds apart (3 times for small prey animals like rabbits and chickens), then one more pat to bring it home. Some kinds differ: birds want you to hold their food out and stand still, big hunters want you to stand your ground, and skittish ones want you to crouch and keep still beside them. Rarer creatures have their own chains, and their clues tell you what they need.

A few of the pack's own creatures have their own way instead: a Gingerbread Buddy is tamed with a cookie, and a Coal Gnome is woken with a Blaze Heart. The Handbook's **Buddies & Pets** page has those.

## Shy and grumpy animals

A wary animal won't let you start until it has warmed up to you. Your thoughts will say so. Toss some of its food near it with **<KeyBind id="key.drop" />**, step back at least 4 blocks and wait. It needs 1 to 3 offerings depending on how wary it is, at least 10 seconds apart. Your thought shows a picture of what it eats. If it eats nothing at all, your thought says "something it likes" and shows that instead. Instead of food you can throw it a toy: it plays with it and bats it back to you. Then give it a pat. This works for the pack's rare creatures too.

A wary animal that you win over is tougher for it: it gets a little extra health and a little extra bite.

## While you're taming

* **The taming bar:** a bar at the top left shows the animal's name and a heart. It fills with right moves and drops a little with wrong ones. It only shows while you're looking at that animal.
* **Keeping track:** a small heart in a thought bubble floats over the animal you're taming, only for you, so you can find it again. You only see it from a few blocks away or more.
* **It stays focused:** once you're working with it and within about 12 blocks, it stops wandering and grazing and keeps facing you. Real danger breaks that, and so does its nature now and then: a skittish or incurious one drifts off for a few seconds.

## Good to know

* You work on one tame per kind of animal at a time. Others of that kind ignore you for taming until you finish or give up.
* An animal you are taming stays nearby and won't wander off. If nothing happens for about 2 in-game days (rarer creatures wait longer), it gives up and goes back to normal. You'll get one line saying it lost interest for now. If you go back within about 5 in-game days, it may remember you ("Looks like it remembers me"). It keeps half to all of your progress, more for a friendly or curious one, but never the final step.
* A finished tame never starts over. Animals the game itself can't tame (cows, pigs, sheep, foxes and so on) become truly yours through Hearthkin. They count for trust perks, the Field Guide and the Pet Whistle, and they never despawn. They look and act the same as ever: lead them with a lead or their food.
* There's no limit on how many rare friends you can keep. Finding them is the hard part.
