# Taming

Any animal with a personality can become a friend, but never by accident. Taming is a short chain of steps, and you always finish it yourself.

## How it starts

Crouch and pet the animal (empty hand), or say yes when it comes to you with a bubble. Then your own thoughts give you a clue about what to try next, using the animal's name. Each step you finish adds a note in your **Field Guide**, and the next clue appears.

Most animals want the same kind of things: let it sniff you, bring its food a couple of times, hold the food out and stand still, then pet it to finish. Rarer creatures have their own chains, and their clues tell you what they need.

## Shy and grumpy animals

A wary animal won't let you start until it has warmed up to you. Your thoughts will say so. Leave some of its food out for it, step well back and wait. Instead of food you can throw it a toy: it plays with it and bats it back to you. Once it has come for the food a few times, try petting it again.

A wary animal that you win over is tougher for it: it gets a little extra health and a little extra bite.

## Good to know

* You work on one tame per kind of animal at a time. Others of that kind ignore you for taming until you finish or give up.
* An animal you are taming stays nearby and won't wander off. If nothing happens for about 2 in-game days (rarer creatures wait longer), it gives up and goes back to normal.
* You can keep up to 5 rare friends; a group counts as one. Your thoughts will tell you if you're at the limit.
