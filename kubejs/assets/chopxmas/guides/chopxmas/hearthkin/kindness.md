# Being Kind (and Not)

## Petting

Crouch and right-click an animal with an **empty hand**. Hearts and a happy line mean it liked that. A few creatures use crouch-click for something else, so they don't get pets that way.

## Pet Treats

The **Pet Treat** mends things. Give one to a pet that is cross with you. It is also an ingredient in the **Field Guide** and the **Temperament Spyglass**.

## Grudges and soft spots

Animals remember you.

* **Soft spot:** feed, pet and play with an animal on different days and it grows fond of you. It follows a little closer and greets you when you come back.
* **Grudge:** hit it, or chase it down at a sprint, and it may hold a grudge and keep its distance. A grudge only ever warns: it never stops you. A treat and some patience mend it.

Every time a grudge or a soft spot forms, you'll see it: a bubble, a few particles and a line at the bottom of your screen.

Trust is counted in days of real time spent together, not minutes standing nearby, so a little kindness every day goes further than a lot at once.
