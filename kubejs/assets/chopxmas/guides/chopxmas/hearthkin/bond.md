# Your Bond

Your pet's bond with you grows through good moments, at most one step a day. There are four steps:

* **Wary**
* **Familiar**
* **Trusted**
* **Kin**: the lasting kind.

There is no bar. You see the bond in how your pet behaves: it follows closer, greets you, and might bring you little gifts. The **Our bond** section on its Field Guide page says where things stand, in your own words ("Biscuit trusts me."), with a hint about what comes next.

Some creatures learn new tricks or perks as your bond grows. Their Field Guide pages hint at what's coming.

## Trust perks

Each pet has a role (fighter, rider, helper, or rare for the pack's rare creatures) that grows with your bond: more health and bite, speed and jump, gifts and a healing pat, and so on. Its nature and quirks add small perks of their own, from a third at Familiar to full at Kin. Everything is modest and capped.

## Pet Whistle

Craft it from a stick and an iron nugget. Use it to call your own rides within 32 blocks to you, once they're Familiar or closer. It plays a short flute note and has a 5 second cooldown.

## Letting a pet go

A **Release Ribbon** (string and paper) lets a pet go free if you no longer want it. It asks you to confirm first.
