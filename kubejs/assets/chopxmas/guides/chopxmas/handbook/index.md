# Chop's Christmas Handbook

Welcome to **Chop's Christmas Special**: a snowy Christmas world full of towns to build, neighbours to meet, creatures to befriend and secrets to find. This book tells you what you need to get started, without giving the surprises away.

## Start here

* **[The Pack at a Glance](start/pack.md):** what this world is about.
* **[Your First Days](start/index.md):** what to do first.
* **[Books Worth Reading](start/library.md):** which other books in your Bookbag to open next, and what each one is for.

## Look things up

* **Controls & Keys:** every key in the pack, showing what it is set to on your game right now. Open **Keybind Atlas** with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />** to change any of them.
* **How Things Work:** the pack's own systems in plain words.
* **Creatures:** where the pack's animals live and what they leave behind.

## Two habits worth having

* **Stuck? Open the quest book.** It always points you at a next step.
* **Not sure what something is?** Look at it: the box at the top of the screen names it and its mod. Press **R** over an item for its recipe, or **U** for what it's used in.
