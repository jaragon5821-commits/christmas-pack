# Chop's Christmas Handbook

Welcome to **Chop's Christmas Special**! This book is your quick guide to the pack.

* **Keys:** open **Keybind Atlas** with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />** to see every key on one keyboard.
* **Festivity:** keep the town jolly. Decorate, feast and give gifts. The Festivity HUD shows how merry things are.
* **Quests:** the quest book has a Christmas chapter. <KeyBind id="key.ftbquests.quests" /> opens it (or the book button in your inventory).
* **Friends:** voice chat works near other players. See the Voice Chat page to pick your mic.
