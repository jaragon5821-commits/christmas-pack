# Chop's Christmas Handbook

Welcome to **Chop's Christmas Special**! This book is your quick guide to the pack.

## Every key in the pack

Every mod's keys are in this book, and each one shows what it is set to on your game right now.

* **[Minecraft Basics](keys/basics.md):** moving, inventory, chat and the everyday keys.
* **[Mod Keys](keys/mods/index.md):** the keys every mod in the pack adds, one page per topic, with each mod under its own heading.
* **Change a key:** open **Keybind Atlas** with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**. It shows every key on one keyboard. Keys marked **Unassigned** have nothing set yet, so you can pick one.

## The rest of the book

* **New here?** Start with **Getting Started** below.
* **Festivity:** keep the town jolly. Decorate, feast and give gifts. The Festivity HUD shows how merry things are.
* **Quests:** the quest book has chapters for Christmas, your colony, the kitchen, getting around, magic, the giants and every dungeon. Open it with the **Quests** button in your inventory.
* **Friends:** voice chat works near other players. See the Voice Chat page to pick your mic.
