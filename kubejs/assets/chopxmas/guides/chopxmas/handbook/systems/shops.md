# Running a Shop

Open your own shop or restaurant, and villagers come in to buy. Look up the blocks in JEI.

## Setting up

1. Place a **Shop Ledger** in your room, right-click it and press **Mark area**. Mark two corners with the Shop Marker, then confirm on the Ledger.
2. Add what your kind of shop needs. The Ledger shows **Ready to open** or a list of what's still missing:
   * **Every shop:** an **Open/Closed Sign** inside the area.
   * **General store:** a **Counter with Register** (the till) and a **Display Shelf**.
   * **Restaurant:** a **Menu Board** with at least one dish, a **Ticket Rail**, a **Ticket Spike**, something to cook on (a furnace, smoker, campfire or stove), and a till or a table with chairs.
   * Leave a free spot in front of every till for customers to stand.
3. Stock your shelves or menu, set prices in emeralds, and flip the sign to **OPEN**.

Customers only come while you're nearby (within 64 blocks of the Ledger). Serve them before they give up, and your shop's **Recognition** grows, which brings more customers.

## Hiring staff

Too busy to stand at the till? Hire help.

1. Open the **Ledger** and press **Staff**.
2. Post a job: **Clerk**, **Stocker** or **Cook**. The Help Wanted notice lasts 3 days.
3. Villagers without a job come in to apply. Hover over one to see what they're like, then press **Hire** or **No**.

| Job | What they do | Pay a day |
|---|---|---|
| **Clerk** | Sells at the counter for you, and keeps selling a little while you're offline. | 3 emeralds |
| **Stocker** | Refills your shelves from chests and barrels inside the shop. | 2 emeralds |
| **Cook** | Cooks with the shop's furnace, smoker or campfire, fills the tickets and serves the diners. | 4 emeralds |

* **Pay day** is every morning. Wages come out of the till (or a Ticket Spike's cash box), so keep some emeralds there. You only pay for days you were online.
* **Unpaid** staff get unhappy, and after 3 days without pay they quit.
* A bigger shop can hold more staff: one per shop level, up to 5.
* Press **Fire** to let someone go. You can't hire the same villager again for 3 days.
* Only the shop's owner can hire.

Wages stay in town: villagers spend them back in the shops. Spending emeralds in town also makes your shop better known.
