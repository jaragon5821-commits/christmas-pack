# Festivity

The whole server shares one **Festivity** bar, from 0 to 100. The HUD shows how merry the world is.

**Raise it:** decorate, eat festive food, give presents, build and grow your colony, feed citizens, trade with villagers, write to Santa, sleep together, and drive off Santy Claws' minions.

**Lower it:** hurt villagers or colonists, kill pets or festive creatures, break decorations someone else placed, blow things up, start fires, or attack other players.

**The bands, from best to worst:**

* Jolly (80+)
* Merry (60 to 79)
* Quiet (50 to 59)
* Grumbling (40 to 49): Santy Claws' minions start coming out at night.
* Bleak (25 to 39)
* Claws' Eve (10 to 24)
* Claws Night (0 to 9): Santy Claws himself comes at midnight. See the Santy Claws page.

Coal Tokens drop from his minions while the bar is 49 or lower. Ribbon Tokens come from rescues and from driving him off.
