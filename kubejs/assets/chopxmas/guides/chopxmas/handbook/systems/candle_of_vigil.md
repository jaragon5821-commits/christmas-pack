# Candle of Vigil

A candle that keeps Santy Claws from breaking into your home.

* **Light it:** right-click it with a **Coal Heart Shard**. It holds up to 4 shards.
* **Fuel:** it burns one shard every dawn. When the shards run out, it goes out.
* **While lit:** Santy Claws can't smash doors or windows, or squeeze through doorways, within **32 blocks** of it.
* It does **not** stop him grabbing you outdoors. Stay inside.

Coal Heart Shards also feed a Clinker Lantern.
