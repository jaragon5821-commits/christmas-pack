# Town Planner

Your town's **Town Planner** board is where you decide what gets built next. Townwright never places a new building without your say-so.

Right-click the board to open the Planning Board: **Plans** (say Yes, No or swap the next building), **Town settings** and the **Plot map**.

The **Townwright Guide** in your Bookbag explains everything: founding a town, paying your builders, roads and bridges, walls, claiming plots for your own builds, and what to do when something seems stuck.
