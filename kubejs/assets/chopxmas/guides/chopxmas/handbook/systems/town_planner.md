# Town Planner & Plan Board

The colony autopilot picks sites and lays roads and bridges, but it **never places a new building without your say-so**.

1. Place your **Town Hall** yourself. Flat ground is cheapest.
2. You get a **Town Planner** board once the hall stands. Put it somewhere handy.
3. Right-click it to see the **plan board**:
   * **Proposal:** what the plan wants next. Click **Accept** or **Skip**.
   * **Or the town could use:** up to 3 other buildings the town needs right now. Hover for the reason, click one to build it instead.
   * **Town wall:** once the town needs defending, the wall waits here until you accept it.
   * **Skipped:** click one to let the plan suggest it again.

**Who decides:** the colony owner and officers. Anyone can look.

**Settings** (right-click the planner): building looks, road type, bridges, snow clearing at build sites, lanterns until guards stand, retaining walls, and town wall style.

**Protect your own builds:** use the **Zone Marker** (`/colonyautopilot zone wand`). Right-click two corners, up to 48 blocks a side. The autopilot builds around it. Sneak-right-click inside a zone to remove it. Needs officer rank.

You can always place huts yourself. The autopilot works around them.
