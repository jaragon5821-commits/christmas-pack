# Emeralds & the Treasury

**Emeralds** are your town's money. With Townwright, builders **buy** their materials from the town's treasury. A new town starts with **56 emeralds** to get the first builds going. After that, keep paying in or the builders wait.

**Getting emeralds:**

* Monsters sometimes drop them, and loot chests hold a few.
* A happy town earns a little every day, more as its huts level up.
* Short? The Town Hall's **Exchange** tab buys diamonds, gold, ancient debris and netherite for emeralds.
* **Old ColonyBucks:** craft each one into 4 emeralds, swap it at the Exchange, or pay it in at the Town Hall. Each counts as 4 emeralds.

**Paying in:**

* Sneak-right-click your **Town Hall** while holding emeralds, or put them in the Town Hall's racks.
* Emeralds in a Warehouse stay there: your citizens may need them as materials.
* Or skip the money and carry the materials to the Builder's Hut yourself.

**Donation Kettle:** place one inside a town and right-click it with emeralds to give 1 (sneak to give 5). They go straight to that town's Town Hall. Your first donation each day also gives you a small 10-minute buff and a little Festivity.

**Stuck?** Type `/townwright status`. It says in plain words what the town is waiting for, for example materials and how many emeralds are in the treasury.
