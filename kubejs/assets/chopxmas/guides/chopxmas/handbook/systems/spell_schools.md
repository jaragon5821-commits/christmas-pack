# Christmas Spell Schools

The pack adds **5 Christmas schools of magic** to Iron's Spells: **Yuletide, Naughty, Toymaker, Hearthfire and Aurora**.

**Where it starts:** each school has a caster mob that drops its **focus**, its **runes** and sometimes a scroll:

* Yuletide: Tinsel Caroler
* Naughty: Coal Trickster
* Toymaker: Clockwork Tinkerer
* Hearthfire: Hearth Keeper
* Aurora: Aurora Seer

**Crafting** (same pattern for every school):

* **Rune:** 8 focuses around a Blank Rune.
* **Upgrade Orb:** 8 runes around an Upgrade Orb. Use it at an Arcane Anvil for +5% of that school's spell power on your gear.
* **Staff:** focus, rune and Arcane Ingot in a column.
* **Spellbook:** runes, Arcane Essence and an Iron Spell Book. It has 8 spell slots.
* **Mage armor:** Magic Cloth and a rune.

The **Spellcraft** quest chapter walks you through it.
