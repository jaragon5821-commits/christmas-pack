# Star Silver & Northstar Gear

Two sets of festive gear, built for the cold.

## Star Silver (about as good as diamond)

* **Finding it:** **Star Silver Ore** sits inside the mountains: frozen, jagged and stony peaks, and snowy slopes. It glows faintly. Mine it with an **iron pickaxe** or better (Fortune works).
* **Smelting:** smelt or blast **Raw Star Silver** into **Star Silver Ingots**.
* **Crafting:** armor and tools use the usual vanilla shapes, with ingots.
* **Built for winter:** every piece keeps you from freezing, and the boots let you walk on powder snow. The pickaxe and shovel cut ice and snow faster.
* **Full set, Starlit:** Frosted can't take hold of you.
* **Star Silver Candy Crook:** its own weapon. Craft it with 3 ingots, a red dye and a stick (look it up in JEI). It has longer reach and hooks what you hit a little closer.

## Northstar (about as tough as netherite)

Northstar upgrades Star Silver at a **smithing table**: a **Northstar Upgrade** template, a Star Silver piece and a **Northstar Ingot**.

* **Northstar Ingot:** 4 Star Silver Ingots, a Mithril Ingot, 2 frost shards (a **Frost Shard** or a Permafrost Shard) and a boss shard: a **Pillar Shard** (from Santy Claws) or a **Blackfrost Shard** (from Old Blackfrost). Blackfrost Shards also make the Blackfrost Core.
* **The template:** sometimes found in the big chests of the Christmas dungeons. Copy it with 7 diamonds and a Block of Star Silver, the same way as netherite's.
* **The catch:** unlike netherite, a dropped Northstar item burns up in lava or fire.
* **Full set, Guiding Star:** a killing blow leaves you at 2 hearts instead, once every 10 minutes. You keep Starlit too.
* **Northstar Morningstar:** upgrade the Candy Crook into it. Critical hits burst on nearby monsters.
