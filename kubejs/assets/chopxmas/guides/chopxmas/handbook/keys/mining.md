# Mining & Building

Hold the Ultimine key while mining to break a whole vein, tree or patch at once: up to **128 blocks** in one go.

**Trees:** there's no automatic tree-feller. Hold Ultimine and break one log to take the whole tree, even the big ones.

**Your keys right now** (says *Not bound* if it has no key yet).

<OptionalSection modId="ftbultimine">

### FTB Ultimine

* Hold to Ultimine: **<KeyBind id="key.ftbultimine" />**
* Next Ultimine shape: **<KeyBind id="ftbultimine.change_shape.next" />**
* Previous Ultimine shape: **<KeyBind id="ftbultimine.change_shape.prev" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="bridgingmod">

### Bridging Mod

Place blocks out in front of you, past the edge you are standing on.

* Toggle easy bridging: **<KeyBind id="key.bridgingmod.toggle_bridging" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="clienttweaks">

### Client Tweaks

* Hold to mine one block only: **<KeyBind id="key.clienttweaks.mineSingleBlock" />**
* Turn log stripping on or off: **<KeyBind id="key.clienttweaks.disableLogStripping" />**

</OptionalSection>

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys ftbultimine,bridgingmod,clienttweaks" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just these mods' keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
