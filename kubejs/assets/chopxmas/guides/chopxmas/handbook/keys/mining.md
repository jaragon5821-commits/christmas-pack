# Mining & Building

Hold the Ultimine key while mining to break a whole vein, tree or patch at once: up to **128 blocks** in one go.

**Trees:** there's no automatic tree-feller. Hold Ultimine and break one log to take the whole tree, even the big ones.

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys ftbultimine,bridgingmod" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just this mod's keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
