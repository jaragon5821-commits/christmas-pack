# Recipes & Info

Look up any recipe, and see what you are looking at.

**Your keys right now** (says *Not bound* if it has no key yet).

<OptionalSection modId="jei">

### JEI (recipes)

* Show or hide the recipe list: **<KeyBind id="key.jei.toggleOverlay" />**
* Recipe for the hovered item: **<KeyBind id="key.jei.showRecipe" />**
* Uses of the hovered item: **<KeyBind id="key.jei.showUses" />**
* Bookmark the hovered item: **<KeyBind id="key.jei.bookmark" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="jade">

### Jade (look-at info)

* Show or hide the look-at info: **<KeyBind id="key.jade.show_overlay" />**
* Hold for more detail: **<KeyBind id="key.jade.show_details" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="guideme">

### GuideME

* Hold over an item to open its guide page: **<KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="immersiveoverlays">

### Immersive Overlays

* Show or hide the extra HUD: **<KeyBind id="key.immersiveoverlays.toggle" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="toastcontrol">

### Toast Control

* Clear pop-up toasts: **<KeyBind id="key.toastcontrol.clear" />**

</OptionalSection>

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys jei,jade,guideme,immersiveoverlays,toastcontrol" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just these mods' keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
