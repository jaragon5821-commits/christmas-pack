# Minecraft Basics

The everyday keys. These are Minecraft's own, so they work the same as in any world.

## Moving

* Walk forward, left, back, right: **<KeyBind id="key.forward" />**, **<KeyBind id="key.left" />**, **<KeyBind id="key.back" />**, **<KeyBind id="key.right" />**
* Jump: **<KeyBind id="key.jump" />**
* Sneak (you won't fall off edges while sneaking): **<KeyBind id="key.sneak" />**
* Sprint: **<KeyBind id="key.sprint" />**

## Hands and items

* Break or hit: **<KeyBind id="key.attack" />**
* Use an item or place a block: **<KeyBind id="key.use" />**
* Pick the block you are looking at: **<KeyBind id="key.pickItem" />**
* Hotbar slots: **<KeyBind id="key.hotbar.1" />** to 9, or scroll the mouse wheel
* Swap with your off hand: **<KeyBind id="key.swapOffhand" />**
* Drop the item in your hand: **<KeyBind id="key.drop" />**
* Inventory: **<KeyBind id="key.inventory" />**

## Talking and looking around

* Chat: **<KeyBind id="key.chat" />**
* Type a command: **<KeyBind id="key.command" />**
* Who's online (hold): **<KeyBind id="key.playerlist" />**
* Change camera view: **<KeyBind id="key.togglePerspective" />**
* Advancements: **<KeyBind id="key.advancements" />**
* Screenshot: **<KeyBind id="key.screenshot" />**
* Full screen: **<KeyBind id="key.fullscreen" />**

## Good to know

* **Shift-click** an item in a chest or your inventory to move it across in one go.
* **Your first night:** make a bed or a shelter before dark. Sleeping makes the night pass faster, and the more players in bed, the faster it goes.
* **You keep your items when you die**, so don't panic. You still lose your spot, so set a bed.
* The **quest book** (**<KeyBind id="key.ftbquests.quests" />**) always shows a next step if you're stuck.
