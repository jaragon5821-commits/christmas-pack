# Spells & Trinkets

Keys for casting spells and for trinkets that switch on and off.

**Your keys right now.** Keys marked **Unassigned** start with no key: click the link to open Keybind Atlas, then pick the key there (Edit, then the Unassigned list).

<OptionalSection modId="irons_spellbooks">

* Hold for the spell wheel: **<KeyBind id="key.irons_spellbooks.spell_wheel" />**
* Spell wheel on or off: **<KeyBind id="key.irons_spellbooks.spell_wheel_toggle" />** · <CommandLink command="/chopkeys" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas">Unassigned: click to assign</CommandLink>

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys irons_spellbooks,relics,artifacts" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just these mods' keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
