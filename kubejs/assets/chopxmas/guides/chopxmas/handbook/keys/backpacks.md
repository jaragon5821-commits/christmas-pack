# Backpacks & Storage

Backpacks hold a lot and take upgrades. Wear one on your back and open it with a key instead of placing it.

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys sophisticatedbackpacks,sophisticatedcore" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just this mod's keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
