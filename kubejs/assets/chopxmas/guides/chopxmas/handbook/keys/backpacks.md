# Backpacks & Storage

Backpacks hold a lot and take upgrades. Wear one on your back and open it with a key instead of placing it.

**Your keys right now** (says *Not bound* if it has no key yet).

<OptionalSection modId="sophisticatedbackpacks">

### Sophisticated Backpacks

* Open the backpack you are wearing: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedbackpacks.open_backpack" />**
* Put the hovered item into the backpack: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedbackpacks.inventory_interaction" />**
* Swap to the best tool from the backpack: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedbackpacks.tool_swap" />**
* Toggle upgrade slot 1: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedbackpacks.toggle_upgrade_1" />**
* Toggle upgrade slot 2: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedbackpacks.toggle_upgrade_2" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="sophisticatedcore">

### Sophisticated Core (storage screens)

* Sort a storage screen: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedcore.sort" />**
* Move everything into the storage: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedcore.transfer_to_storage" />**
* Move everything into your inventory: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedcore.transfer_to_inventory" />**

</OptionalSection>

<OptionalSection modId="sophisticatedsorter">

### Sophisticated Sorter

Works in chest and container screens.

* Sort the open container: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedsorter.sort" />**
* Hide the sort buttons on this container: **<KeyBind id="key.sophisticatedsorter.disable" />**

</OptionalSection>

## Set your own keys

<CommandLink command="/chopkeys sophisticatedbackpacks,sophisticatedcore,sophisticatedsorter" close={true} title="Opens Keybind Atlas showing only these keys">Show these keys in Keybind Atlas</CommandLink>

Atlas opens showing just these mods' keys that already have a key. Keys marked *Not bound* above are in Atlas under **Edit** (pencil) then **Unassigned**: type the mod name in its search, click one, press the key you want, then **Apply**.
You can also open Keybind Atlas any time with **<KeyBind id="key.keybindatlas.open_keyboard" />**.
