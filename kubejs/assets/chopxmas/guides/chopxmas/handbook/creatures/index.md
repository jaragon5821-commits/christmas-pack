# Creatures

A field book of the pack's own creatures: where they live and what they leave behind. Vanilla mobs are not listed here.

* **[Wildlife](wildlife.md):** animals of the snow, the forests and the cold seas.
* **[Things That Bite](dangers.md):** creatures out in the open that you should watch for.
* **[Who Lives in the Ruins](ruins.md):** the folk you meet inside camps, ships, dungeons and other places.

Bosses are not in this book. The quest book's **Dungeons** chapters cover them.

For how animals think and feel, see **Animals & Personality** under **Getting Started**.
