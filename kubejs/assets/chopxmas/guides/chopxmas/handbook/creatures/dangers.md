# Things That Bite

* **Christmas Creeper:** can turn up anywhere in the Overworld. Drops gunpowder, Candy Canes and Gumdrops.
* **Frost Hound:** snowy lands, in packs of 2 or 3. Drops bones and snowballs. If you killed it, it may also drop blue ice, a **Frost Pelt** or a **Frost Shard**.
* **The Listener:** wanders snowy lands alone. Drops bones and string.

## Wandering spellcasters

Five kinds walk the snowy and cold lands alone, one for each Christmas school of magic: the **Tinsel Caroler** (Yuletide), **Coal Trickster** (Naughty), **Clockwork Tinkerer** (Toymaker), **Hearth Keeper** (Hearthfire) and **Aurora Seer** (Aurora). Each school also has its own spell tower.

Beat one and it drops 1 or 2 runes of its school and some arcane essence. Sometimes you also get a scroll, the school's focus or an upgrade orb.
