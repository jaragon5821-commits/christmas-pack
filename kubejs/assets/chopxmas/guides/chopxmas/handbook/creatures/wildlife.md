# Wildlife

## In the snow

* **Reindeer:** herds of 2 to 4 in snowy lands, smaller groups in other cold places. Drops leather and Raw Venison.
* **Arctic Hare:** snowy lands, alone or in small groups. Drops rabbit hide and rabbit.
* **Christmas Robin:** snowy lands, in ones and twos.
* **Snow Goose:** snowy lands, in flocks of 3 to 6. Drops feathers and Raw Goose.
* **Snowy Owl:** snowy lands, always alone and not often seen. Drops feathers.
* **Frostpuff:** snowy lands, in twos and threes. Drops Frosty Fluff.

## In the forests and taiga

* **Forest Deer:** forests and taiga, in small herds. Drops leather and Raw Venison.
* **Turkey:** forests, in groups of 2 to 4. Drops feathers and Raw Turkey.
* **Squirrel:** forests and taiga. Drops Hazelnuts.
* **Boar:** forests and taiga. Drops porkchops and sometimes leather.
* **Raccoon:** forests. Sometimes drops rabbit hide.
* **Hedgehog:** forests.

## High in the mountains

* **Snow Leopard:** snowy slopes, groves and frozen or jagged peaks. Always alone, and rare. Drops leather.

## By the water

* **Penguin:** frozen oceans, snowy beaches, frozen rivers and ice spikes, in groups of 2 to 4. Drops feathers.
* **Harp Seal:** the same icy shores as the penguins. Drops leather.
* **Sea Otter:** rivers, frozen rivers, cold oceans, stony shores and snowy beaches. Sometimes drops rabbit hide.
* **Beaver:** rivers and swamps. Sometimes drops rabbit hide.
* **Narwhal:** cold and frozen oceans. Drops cod, and sometimes a **Narwhal Tusk** if you killed it.

## Fish

* **Arctic Char:** cold and frozen oceans, in schools.
* **Frostfin:** frozen oceans. Sometimes drops glowstone dust as well.
* **Snow Smelt:** frozen rivers, in schools of 3 to 6.
