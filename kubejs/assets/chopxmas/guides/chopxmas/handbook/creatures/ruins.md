# Who Lives in the Ruins

These creatures don't wander in from the wild. You meet them inside places.

* **Sour Elves:** the most common trouble. Hideouts, outposts, Claws' camps and ships, dungeons, and even the Nether and the End.
* **Nutcrackers:** stand guard in fortresses, cult compounds, tombs, dungeons, and Claws' holds in the Nether and the End. Drop iron nuggets and sticks. If you killed one, it may drop red dye or a **Brass Gear**.
* **Coal Gnomes:** mineheads, Claws' camps and ships, dungeons, and many places in the Nether.
* **Frost Hounds:** besides the wild packs, Claws keeps them at his camps on the ice and the sea.
* **Frostbitten Carolers:** drowned freighters and workshops, catacombs and other cold, sad places. Drop rotten flesh and snowballs.
* **Gingerbread Men:** hot spring mounds, snow barrows and the giant cast-off things you find out in the world, like a lost mitten or a cocoa mug.
* **Claws Divers:** only around Claws' sea bases, from wrecks to dredge rigs.
