# Magic

Spells are written on scrolls and copied into a spell book at an **Inscription Table**. Make your own scrolls at a **Scroll Forge**.

The pack adds five festive schools of magic:

* **Yuletide:** ornaments, tinsel and wrapping paper turned into weapons.
* **Naughty:** coal, pranks and Krampus chains.
* **Toymaker:** wind-up soldiers, nutcrackers and toy trains that fight for you.
* **Hearthfire:** warmth, feasts and gingerbread golems. The healer's school.
* **Aurora:** northern lights, shooting stars and the long polar night.

On snowy nights each school's **casters** wander the cold. Beat one for its **focus** and **runes**, then craft that school's spellbook, staff, robes and upgrade orb. The **Spellcraft** quest chapter shows the way.
