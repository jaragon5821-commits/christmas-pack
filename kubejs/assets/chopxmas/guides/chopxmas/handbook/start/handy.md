# Handy Things

* **Recipes (JEI):** the item list on the right of your inventory. Hover an item and press **R** for its recipe or **U** for what it's used in.
* **What am I looking at? (Jade):** the box at the top of the screen names the block or creature you're looking at and the mod it comes from.
* **Hunger (AppleSkin):** shows how much a food will fill you up.
* **Ultimine:** hold the Ultimine key to break up to 128 blocks at once: ore veins, dirt patches, whole trees. See **Mining & Building** under Controls & Keys.
* **Item clumping:** dropped items pull together into full stacks.
* **Carry On:** pick up chests and small animals with an empty hand while crouching.
* **Backpacks:** Sophisticated Backpacks hold far more than your pockets, and can be upgraded.
* **Iron Furnaces:** faster furnaces. Craft one, or upgrade a furnace you've already placed.
* **Your own loot:** every player gets their own loot from each chest, so nobody empties a chest before you get there.
