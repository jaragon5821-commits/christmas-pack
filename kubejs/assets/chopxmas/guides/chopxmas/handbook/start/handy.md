# Handy Things

* **Item clumping:** dropped items pull together into full stacks, so a felled tree's saplings land as one pile, not twenty.
* **Ultimine:** hold the Ultimine key to break up to **128 blocks** at once: ore veins, dirt patches, whole trees. See **Mining & Building** under Controls & Keys.
* **Iron Furnaces:** faster furnaces (Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond and up). Craft one, or use an **Upgrade** on a furnace you've already placed. There's even a Christmas skin. The **Kitchen & Farm** quests walk you through it.
