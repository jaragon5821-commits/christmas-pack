# Animals & Personality

Many of the pack's animals and pets have a **personality**: a nature of their own, moods that come and go, and a memory for how you treat them. You will not see numbers. You read them the way you would a real animal.

## Reading an animal

* **Mood bubbles:** a small bubble over an animal's head shows a strong mood. A calm, content animal shows nothing, which is a good sign.
* **The Temperament Spyglass:** craft one from a **spyglass** and a **Pet Treat**. Hold right-click and aim at an animal. Its thoughts drift up beside your crosshair, hinting at whether it is friendly, shy or one to keep an eye on. It works on wild animals and pets.
* **The Field Guide:** craft one from a **book** and a **Pet Treat**, then right-click to open it. It is your own pet journal. Every pet you tame gets a page with **Today**, **Its nature** and **Memories**, and a **Rename** button. When you tame a pet, a card asks if you want to name it now or later.

## Being kind

* **Petting:** crouch and right-click your own pet with an empty hand. Hearts and a happy line mean it liked that. A few pets use crouch-click for something else (Lava Lizard, Field Mouse, Coal Gnome, Christmas Creeper, Snowman, Gingerbread Buddy and Giant, and a Frostpuff pulling a sled), so they don't get pets that way.
* **Hitting:** an animal you hit lets you know with a grumpy bubble, angry puffs and a line at the bottom of your screen.
* **Chasing:** wild animals that you chase down at a sprint may hold a grudge and keep their distance.

The **Friends & Family** quests walk you through the Pet Treat, the Field Guide and the Spyglass.
