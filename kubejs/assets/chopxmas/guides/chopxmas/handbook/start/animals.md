# Animals & Personality

Many of the pack's animals have a **personality**: a nature of their own, moods that come and go, and a memory for how you treat them. You won't see numbers; you read them the way you would a real animal.

* An animal that walks up to you may show a **thought bubble** with what it wants: food, pets, play, shelter.
* **Pet** an animal by crouching and right-clicking it with an empty hand.
* Animals remember kindness, and they remember being chased or hit.
* Taming is a few friendly steps, never an accident, and your own thoughts give you the clues.

The **Hearthkin Guide** in your Bookbag explains it all. The **Friends & Family** quests walk you through the Pet Treat, the Field Guide and the Temperament Spyglass.
