# Books Worth Reading

Your **Bookbag** holds every guide book in the pack. Open it with **<KeyBind id="key.festivity.bookbag" />** while you wear it. Here's what to read, and when.

## Read first

* **This Handbook:** the pack, your first days, and every key.
* **The Quest Book:** your to-do list and your teacher. Open it from your inventory. Start with **Start Here**.

## When you start a town

* **Townwright Guide:** how your town plans and builds itself, how to pay your builders, and what to do if something seems stuck.
* **The MineColonies huts:** each hut explains itself when you right-click it.

## When you meet the animals

* **Hearthkin Guide:** reading an animal's mood, answering what it wants, petting, taming and bonds.
* **Field Guide:** your own pet journal, taming notes and the creatures you've seen.

* **Animal Pen Guide Book:** keeping farm animals in pens.

## When you get to know the villagers

* **The villager books (MCA):** how to talk to villagers, give gifts, court, marry and raise a family.

## For the season

* **Festive Book:** all the ways to raise or lower Festivity. Its entries fill in as you discover them.
* **Merry-Maker's Manual:** the guide to Mama's Merry Making and its festive things.

## In the kitchen

* **Festive Cookbook** and **Lunchtime's Little Cookbook:** recipes to try. The **Kitchen & Farm**, **Christmas Baking** and **Winter Pantry** quest chapters lead the way.

## Later on

* **Powah's guide:** power generators, cables and machines, for when you want electricity.
* **Iron's Guidebook:** how spells, scrolls and spell books work. The **Spellcraft** quest chapter takes you through the Christmas schools.
* **Jewelcrafting and You:** making rings and amulets with gems.

## Found in the world

Old books, notes and torn pages turn up in chests and ruins. They tell the story of this place a little at a time, and some of them hint at things worth looking for. Keep them: your Bookbag has a library shelf for them.
