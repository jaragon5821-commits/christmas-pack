# Flying

Tamed reindeer can fly, and so can a sleigh pulled by them.

## On a reindeer

Put a **Flying Saddle** on your own tamed, grown-up reindeer. It works as an ordinary saddle on the ground too. To take it off, sneak and use shears on the reindeer (only its owner can).

## In a Flying Sleigh

The **Flying Sleigh** seats two. Lead your own tamed, grown-up reindeer to it (from within 12 blocks) and use the sleigh to hitch them, up to two. One reindeer pulls at about three quarters speed; with none, the sleigh won't move. A hitched team needs no Flying Saddle.

* Only the owner and their team can drive or unhitch it. Anyone can ride behind a driver.
* To unhitch, make sure nobody is aboard, then sneak and use the sleigh.

Look up both recipes in JEI.

## Flying it

* **<KeyBind id="key.jump" />:** take off, and flap for a boost. Flaps use stamina (the bar above your hotbar).
* **<KeyBind id="key.forward" />:** build speed. Let go and you glide.
* **<KeyBind id="key.back" />** or **<KeyBind id="key.sprint" />:** brake.
* **Mouse left and right:** bank, and the bank turns you. **Mouse up and down:** nose up or down. Let go and it levels out.
* **<KeyBind id="key.left" />** / **<KeyBind id="key.right" />:** a gentle rudder.
* **<KeyBind id="key.sneak" />:** get off. Leave in mid-air and you float down with Slow Falling for 10 seconds.
* **<KeyBind id="key.togglePerspective" />:** a third-person view helps.

Fly low or touch the ground to land.
