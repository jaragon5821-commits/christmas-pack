# Festivity

The town remembers how you treat it. That memory is called **Festivity**, and the bar at the top of the screen shows it.

* **Raises it:** helping villagers, giving gifts, donating, decorating, feasting.
* **Lowers it:** hurting villagers, griefing, stealing.
* Newcomers get a grace period.

**Nice or naughty?** Nice players get warm welcomes and better trades. Naughty players end up on Santy Claws's list... and he pays in coal.
