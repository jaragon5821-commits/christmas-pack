# Festivity

The whole server shares one **Festivity** bar, shown at the top of the screen. Everything everyone does nudges it up or down.

* **Raises it:** helping villagers, giving gifts, donating, decorating, feasting.
* **Lowers it:** hurting villagers, griefing, stealing.
* Newcomers get a grace period.

**Nice or naughty?** That part is personal: you each have your own place on the list. Nice players get warm welcomes and better trades. Naughty players end up on Santy Claws's list... and he pays in coal.
