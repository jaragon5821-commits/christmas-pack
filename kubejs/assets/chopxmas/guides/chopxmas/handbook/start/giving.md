# Giving

Right-click a **Donation Kettle** with emeralds to give them to that town (sneak to give 5). Giving raises your Festivity.

From **December 1st**, open one **Advent Calendar** door a day.

## Lost letters

Now and then a little paper crane lands to rest, tired out, especially in rain or snow. Right-click it to unfold the letter it carries and read it.

* **Send it north:** the crane flies on its way, and your Festivity rises a little.
* **Keep it:** the crane stays where it is for now.

Press Esc to decide later. You can read the same letter again until the crane leaves.
