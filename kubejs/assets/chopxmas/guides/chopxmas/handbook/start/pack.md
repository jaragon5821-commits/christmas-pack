# The Pack at a Glance

**Chop's Christmas Special** runs on Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge. Here's what you'll find, in broad strokes.

## A winter world

Snowy forests, frozen peaks, icy coasts and new biomes from Biomes O' Plenty, Oh The Biomes We've Gone and Tectonic. The seasons turn (Serene Seasons), the moon has moods of its own, and Christmas runs through all of it.

## Towns and neighbours

* **MineColonies** lets you found a town that your citizens build and run. **Townwright** plans its streets, roads and walls with you. See the **Townwright Guide**.
* **Villagers** (Minecraft Comes Alive) have names, families, jobs and lives of their own. You can befriend them, help them, even marry. MCA Quests gives them jobs for you.

## Festivity

The pack's own heart. The whole server shares one **Festivity** bar: how merry the world is. Kindness, decorating, feasting and giving raise it. Cruelty and destruction lower it. Keep it high, or the season turns darker.

## Creatures

New animals live in the snow, the forests and the cold seas, and a few rare ones are only ever glimpsed. Many have a personality (Hearthkin). Some you can tame, ride or bring home.

## Adventure

Christmas ruins, giant statues, lost camps, wrecks and dungeons wait out in the world, with loot worth the trip. Some places are best visited with friends.

## Making things

* **Cooking and farming:** Farmer's Delight and its friends, Let's Do, festive foods and a whole Christmas kitchen.
* **Magic:** Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, plus five Christmas schools of magic.
* **Power and storage:** Powah for energy, Refined Storage and Sophisticated Storage for your things.
* **Building:** Chipped, Handcrafted, Macaw's, Supplementaries and many more blocks to decorate with.

## Playing together

Voice chat works near other players. Teams, party claims and shared waypoints help groups play side by side.
