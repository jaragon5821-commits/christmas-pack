# Your First Days

You start with a **Bookbag** full of books (this Handbook among them) and not much else. Everything else you find, craft or earn.

1. **Get through the first night.** Punch a tree, make tools and find shelter before dark. It's winter out there: the cold and the long nights are real.
2. **Open the quest book** with the quest button in your inventory. The **Start Here** chapter walks you through your first steps and points you at the next one.
3. **Find a place to settle.** When you're ready, start your town (see **Your Town**). Your townsfolk will build it with you.
4. **Meet the neighbours.** Villagers have names, moods, families and stories. Talk to them, help them, give them gifts.
5. **Keep the season merry.** Decorating, feasting and giving all lift the **Festivity** bar at the top of the screen. The world is kinder when it's high.
6. **Make friends with the animals.** Many of them have a personality and a memory for how you treat them.

There's no rush. Explore, build, and see what the snow is keeping.
